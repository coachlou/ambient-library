# The Quality Gate Rubric

Force self-evaluation at every handoff: generate an artifact-specific rubric, score against it, revise up to 3 times until 9/10, or hand off with a flagged failure. The mechanism that made Lou's live Brand Writing Team build score 9s end-to-end (Apr 9, 2026).

---

For every significant handoff or stage in the workflow below, I want you to stop and self-evaluate before moving forward.

---

Here's how the gate works:

1. Before each handoff, generate a short evaluation rubric specific to what just got produced. The rubric should have 4–6 criteria, each scored 1–10. Criteria must be specific to the artifact, not generic ("clarity" or "quality" alone is not enough — make it concrete: "does each claim cite a verifiable source," "does the tone match [VOICE]," etc.).

2. Score the current output against the rubric. Show me the scores and a one-line justification per criterion.

3. If the total average is below 9/10, revise the output and rescore. Repeat up to 3 times, or until you hit 9/10, whichever comes first.

4. If you stop at 3 revisions without hitting 9/10, hand off anyway but flag which criteria are still below bar and why you couldn't lift them. Do not silently accept mediocre output.

5. Only after passing the gate — or explicitly flagging the failure — move to the next stage.

Apply this to every stage automatically. Do not ask me to approve individual gates unless you hit the revision limit.
