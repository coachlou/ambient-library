# The Self-Brief Planner

Force Claude to think before it solves, surface the research, questions, decisions, and documents you actually need before diving into answers. From the May 28, 2026 AIMM session, Lou's reframe for Joanna: stop asking for answers one at a time and start by asking what you need to know.

---

$ARGUMENTS

If no situation or goal was provided above, ask me to describe it before proceeding.

Don't solve it yet. First, think it through and tell me:
- What research do I need to do?
- What questions do I need to answer?
- What positions or decisions do I need to take?
- What documents or materials should I have on hand?

Lay it all out as a plan. Once I've reviewed it and confirmed, I'll ask you to execute the parts I hand off, but I want to see the thinking and approve the approach first.
