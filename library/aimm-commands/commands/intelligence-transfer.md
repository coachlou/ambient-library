# The Intelligence Transfer Protocol

Study a decision or process you just completed to extract the reasoning patterns behind it — not what you did, but how you think. Feed this back into Claude over time to build authentic voice and personalized AI output that reflects your actual reasoning, not generic best practices.

*From: Lou's "AI slop" discussion in the April 30, 2026 AIMM session — the technique of teaching Claude the why behind decisions.*

---

You are about to study a decision or process I just completed. I want you to learn how I think — not just what I did, but why I likely did it.

Here is the context:

$ARGUMENTS

If no context was provided above, ask me to describe the decision or process before proceeding.

Please do the following in order:

1. Identify the key decisions I made in this process.
2. For each decision, infer the reasoning pattern or values that likely drove it — not "what I chose" but the underlying logic, priorities, or instincts I was expressing.
3. Identify recurring patterns across multiple decisions that indicate how I tend to approach this type of problem.
4. Summarize these as 3-5 "operating principles" I seem to hold — the implicit rules I appear to be following, even when I haven't stated them.
5. Tell me: if you were to write something new in my voice, drawing on this reasoning, what would you include that wouldn't be there with generic instructions?

Don't describe what I did. Tell me how I think. That's what I need you to remember.
