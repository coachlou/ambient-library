# Format: promo

A short teaser or ad that earns a click.

- **Sources:** the one headline the user wants to land (ask only if nothing gives it), the
  landing page, and the most striking screen or result in the app.
- **Brief defaults:** audience is cold (social feed, ad slot); goal is one action (visit, sign
  up, update); length 15-45 s, 4-7 shots of 3-6 s; tone punchy but not shouted (`[upbeat]`,
  `[confident]`, `[playfully]`); aspect from where it runs: 16:9 for YouTube or a site, 9:16
  for Reels/Shorts/TikTok (cards: `viewport` [900, 1600], `width` 1080), 1:1 for a feed post.
- **Beats:** a hook in the first 3 s, two or three proof shots, the call to action.
- **Shot structure:** hook card or striking screen, 2-4 fast app or card shots with one ring
  each, a closing card with the call to action and the URL on screen.
- **Script:** at most 12 words per shot; the hook is a question or a sharp claim. Captions on
  every shot, since most feeds play muted.
- **Music:** louder than other formats (`musicGain` 0.9-1.0); a user-supplied track if they
  have a brand one.
