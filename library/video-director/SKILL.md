---
name: video-director
description: Use this skill when the user wants a narrated video made end to end — a release or "what's new" video, a tutorial or how-to, a product demo, a promo or teaser, an explainer, or any other narrated screen or slide video — in their own (ElevenLabs) voice or a stock one, with screen captures or title cards, callout rings on what the narration names, a music bed, and an mp4 out. It directs the whole job itself: writes a requirements brief (audience, length, tone, sources, deliverable), picks a format protocol when one fits or the general process when none does, then researches, scripts, captures, voices, scores, edits and checks the video. Triggers include "make a release video for v1.2", "a launch video for this version", "a tutorial video on setting up X", "a two-minute demo of the app", "a 30-second teaser", "an explainer video on how Y works", "redo the video with callouts", "add voice-over to the demo", or reusing the voice style from an earlier video.
---

Read `instructions.md` in this skill's directory and follow it.

Path note: this skill also ships inside the `ambient` library plugin, so its
instructions may reference files as `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/library/video-director/<file>`.
When installed standalone, resolve those to `<file>` in this directory.
